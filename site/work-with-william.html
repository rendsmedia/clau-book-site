Experience

Built on delivery, not theory

As Head of Land Acquisition at the Uganda National Roads Authority until December 2020, William led the team that brought land acquisition in-house. A study of 45 road projects found the cost of acquiring land fell by 64.5% and the time taken by 53.2%.

Earlier, he headed land surveying during the transformation of Kampala City Council into KCCA. He is a fellow of the Institute of Surveyors of Uganda and a certified trainer of trainers in financial literacy.

  • 149 national road projectsLand acquisition and resettlement across Uganda's road network
  • 75,000+ householdsLivelihoods supported through compensation and resettlement
  • ≈ 400,000 project affected personsReached through disclosure, payment and grievance processes
  • 15+ yearsIn land surveying, land management and acquisition
